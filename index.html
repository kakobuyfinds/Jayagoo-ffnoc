03 / A practical method

Less scrolling.
Better comparisons.

A useful Jayagoo clothing spreadsheet should help you make a decision. Keep the fields simple, use consistent measurements, and flag missing information.

Find your starting point ↗

  1. Choose a category

    Start with shoes, clothing, or accessories. Decide whether you need an everyday staple, a seasonal layer, or a finishing detail.

  2. Record listing details

    Save the product link, selected variant, displayed price, size measurements, and material information. Add a date so you know when you checked.

  3. Check the evidence

    Compare available product photos, measurements, seller information, and return conditions. Mark missing information for follow-up instead of guessing.

  4. Estimate the total

    Account for item price, applicable service fees, shipping, and destination charges before deciding. Treat estimates as provisional until confirmed.

05 / Before you browse

Clear answers.
Useful context.

A few details to help you use this guide and evaluate product listings more carefully.

What is a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Jayagoo spreadsheet means a shopping research sheet for organizing product links, categories, variants, size notes, and estimated costs. This page is an independent browsing guide, not an official Jayagoo service or a downloadable live spreadsheet.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS categories. General browse buttons and topic links open the CAFANS homepage.

How should I compare shoe and clothing sizes?

For shoes, compare the listing’s size chart and available foot-length guidance. For clothing, compare garment measurements with a similar item you own. Do not assume that the same labeled size fits identically across listings.

Does a brand name or photograph prove authenticity?

No. A brand name, logo, or photograph alone does not verify authenticity, seller authorization, or product quality. Review the actual listing, seller evidence, and applicable purchase protections.

Are the displayed prices and shipping costs guaranteed?

This page does not publish live prices or shipping quotes. Check current listing details and obtain a destination-specific estimate before purchase.

Is this website affiliated with Jayagoo or the featured brands?

This is an independent CAFANS browsing and research guide. It does not claim affiliation with or endorsement by Jayagoo, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès.
